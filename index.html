Welcome to the Singleton Strikers

Proudly serving our community since 1971, Singleton Strikers Football Club offers football programs from MiniRoos to all age groups, catering to both social and semi-professional players.

We are committed to fostering talent, promoting sportsmanship, and providing a welcoming environment for all members.
